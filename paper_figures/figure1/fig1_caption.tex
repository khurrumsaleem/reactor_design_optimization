\caption{\textbf{Scientific feedback beyond training conventions in two implementations and the attribution of the adaptation.}
The header names the stages shared by both implementations, a training corpus that fixes a design convention, a policy, generated candidates and a scientific evaluator whose feedback updates the policy through DPO or GRPO.
\textbf{a} Reactor lattices. A $17\times17$ training lattice, one of 10{,}000 OpenMC-evaluated lattices that all contain 16 absorber rods, is serialized cell by cell into 289 symbols for fuel (\texttt{f}), absorber (\texttt{g}) and guide channel (\texttt{c}) in raster order. The histogram gives the multiplication factor of the corpus, whose nearest lattice lies 11{,}192 pcm above the requested target of 1.05. Gemma 3 with 270 million parameters, initialized by continued pre-training and supervised fine-tuning on this corpus, generates text for the prompt $k=1.05$, $F_q=F_{\Delta H}=1.00$. The text is corrected to 289 symbols and evaluated by OpenMC. The generated lattice shown is the selected DPO design with 33 absorbers, cut at mid-height. The two OpenMC maps give the thermal neutron flux ($E<0.625$ eV) relative to its maximum and the pin fission rate relative to the mean over all 289 cells for the same evaluation ($k_\infty=1.0492$, one million particles). The objective combines power peaking with the target error.
\textbf{b} Reaction conditions. The supervised corpus keeps one anchor ligand per aryl-halide task (filled cells), one quarter of the task--ligand pairs of the published Buchwald--Hartwig catalog of 3{,}955 measured reactions over 15 tasks, four ligands, 22 additives and three bases. A categorical policy with hidden size 48 selects ligand, additive and base in sequence under a mask restricting generation to measured rows. The coupling uses fixed p-toluidine. Colours show measured yields for 3-bromopyridine with MTBD across the four ligands (rows, in catalog order XPhos, t-BuXPhos, t-BuBrettPhos and AdBrettPhos) and 22 additives. The reward is the negative absolute yield error.
\textbf{c} Attribution on the absorber-inventory axis. Outlined bars give the inventory of 5{,}000 corrected outputs from the five supervised policies before alignment, 1{,}000 per seed-specific CPT$+$SFT checkpoint under the same prompt, decoding and correction as the aligned audit. Of these, 88.6\% contain exactly 16 absorbers and none contain more than 18. The tallest bar is clipped. The triangle marks the corpus, in which every lattice has 16 absorbers. Grey bars give the inventory of 10{,}000 corrected, unselected outputs from the ten final single-target checkpoints (DPO and GRPO pooled), of which 5.2\% exceed 64. The dashed line is the density of the inventory-uniform sampler over 0--264 on the same scale, which reaches the productive inventories (availability). The upper bracket marks 28--35, the inventories of the ten selected aligned layouts, which also contain every target-window candidate of both random procedures. The aligned distribution concentrates there (redistribution), and the arrow marks the objective-removal control, in which four of five matched runs select zero absorbers once the target term is removed. The lower bracket marks the interval 20--40 supplied to informed random search (information value). Right, best-of-budget objectives after 2{,}000 evaluations as five-run means with sample standard deviations for inventory-uniform random search, informed random search and the two aligned policies, followed by the exact catalog probability on ligands excluded from task-specific training and the expected absolute yield error at requested yield 90, as five-seed means for the supervised base, DPO and GRPO. Dashed lines mark the full-catalog uniform sampler.}
